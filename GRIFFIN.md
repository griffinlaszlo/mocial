## How he talks

The best sample of his voice is his real PhD statement of purpose
(`PHD_purpose_of_intent.pdf`). Read it before writing anything. What it shows:

- **The bit goes on past where it should end, and that's the joke.** Nine
  sign-offs in a row ("God Bless Our Troops, / Always thinking of you, /
  Praying for your good health, / Feel free to text me anytime"), then a
  P.S., then another line after the P.S.
- **Huge, deadpan claims.** "I intend to reinvent the wheel." "If you admit
  him, you will be guaranteed luck for the next 10 years." Said with a
  straight face, never with a wink.
- **Switching between the narrator and himself.** Opens in third person
  like a fairy tale ("Once upon a time... a boy named Griffin"), then: "Now
  it is Griffin speaking."
- **Sincere underneath.** The joke never cancels the warmth: "just know
  that God loves you (and so do I)."
- **Nerd humor mixed in casually.** The git command shouted in an airport.
  He jokes from inside the field, not about it.

From `ideas.txt` (his own notes, signed "- Griffin"): when he's not doing a
bit he's lowercase and quick, with real observations ("I get into WWIII rabbit
holes on IG constantly at work. It's dangerous."), "idk", and a last item that's
just "Loading...".

From the sites: labels that undersell or misdirect ("our competition",
"about us", "free ideas", "sneaky links", "darkness"); "take it to
conversion town"; "Loading one of the greatest songs in history..."; tips
like "The dino on the horizon has been walking since 1998. He has not
arrived yet. Please do not tell him."

**Do:**
- Short sentences. Deadpan. Let the absurd part sit there without explaining it.
- Specific, physical details (ski boots on sand, a lava lamp that stares back).
- One sincere line per page, unguarded.
- Use the real thing when one exists. A real song, a real stat, a real photo.

**Don't:**
- Sound like LinkedIn. The old bio in `archive-old-content.txt` is the
  anti-example: "My professional journey is marked by a series of exciting
  projects..." Griffin would never say "pivotal role." It even ends by
  accidentally addressing him ("your professional background"), which tells
  you it wasn't written by him.
- Wink, add "lol," or explain the joke.
- Use exclamation points sincerely. In SPAM-ad parody, as many as you want.

## How his brain works

- **Takes the premise all the way.** The site isn't "retro-styled." It *is*
  a 1998 desktop, with a taskbar, spam pop-ups, a screensaver, a webring, a
  guestbook and a hit counter. If the world has a rule, everything follows it.
- **Makes it real.** A working chairlift, not set dressing. A guestbook in a
  real database. A hit counter that counts real visits. A game that actually
  gives away the password.
- **Hides things for people who poke around.** Drag the home icon into the
  header to unlock settings. Win hangman or roll double sixes to get the
  password. Hover griffin on the lift and he raises a beer.
- **Space and continuity matter.** Windows fly out of the icon that opened
  them and shrink back into it. Going from mocial to griffinland happens in
  the same tab, so "it feels like the desktop changed under you."
- **Tunes by feel, in rounds.** The world-clock went from popup to tray
  flyout over two rounds of "that's not quite it." Expect to iterate. Show,
  don't describe.
- **Engineer and showman together.** The funny version and the technically
  good version are the same version. *(my read)*
- **Ideas everywhere.** Barbers who are also licensed therapists ("Herapy"),
  TikTok for history lessons, Hot or Not for outfits. The "free ideas"
  window exists because he has more ideas than time. *(my read)*

## Taste

- **The look:** Win95/98. Chrome title bars, pixel icons, sunken tray boxes,
  88x31 badges, WordArt, animated gifs (spinning world, lava lamp, dancing,
  fire, cheetah). mocial's desktop is black; griffinland's is forest green
  with a white treeline and a chairlift.
- **Motion:** playful but purposeful, and it stops for visitors who've
  asked for reduced motion.
- **How it's built:** no framework, no build step. Plain HTML/CSS/JS, jQuery
  where it already is. Static files on GitHub Pages.
